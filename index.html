About

Hi, I'm Nissa. I build the part of your business nobody sees.

For more than six years I've worked behind the scenes of online businesses: coaches, consultants, course creators and podcasters.

The work I kept getting pulled into was always the messy kind. A CRM nobody trusted. A funnel that never sent the follow-up. A course where every new student meant ten manual steps.

I liked fixing those: mapping how things should flow, building it, and watching it run without anyone touching it. So I made systems my focus.

Today I build websites, CRMs, funnels, automations and course platforms, mostly in GoHighLevel. I explain things in plain English, and I build systems you can keep running after I'm done.

Practical enough to keep it simple.
